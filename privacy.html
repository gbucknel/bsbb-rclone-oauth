Privacy policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026

Purpose and access

BSBB rclone sync is a private, single-user utility. It requests read-only access to Google Drive solely to list and download sheet-music files that its operator is already authorised to access.

Use and storage

Downloaded files are stored only on a band-owned Raspberry Pi so musicians can access them on a local network, including when no internet connection is available. OAuth credentials are stored locally on that appliance.

Sharing and retention

The utility does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or transmit it to unrelated third parties. Local copies are retained for the operational purpose described above and may be removed by the operator.

Control

Access can be revoked at any time through the Google Account security settings. Revoking access prevents further Google Drive synchronization.
